# 38. v1.2 acceptance exclusions

Date: 2026-10-28
Status: Accepted

## Context

The v1.1.0 exclusions in ADR 0036 do not automatically apply to later releases.
The v1.2.0 feature plan requires physical-machine verification and independent
reviews before publication. The available physical Mac runs macOS 27.0.1.

## Decision

The owner explicitly extends the two acceptance exclusions to v1.2.0:
actual VoiceOver operation and macOS 26 VM visual interaction are unverified
and excluded from publication gates. Do not describe either as passed.

All other checks remain required. The owner separately auditioned the actual
app's human-speech speed output and reported no problem. Public release is
authorized after the remaining checks and reviews pass, with CI still disabled.

## Consequences

Publish local build, test, lint, device and review evidence together with these
limits. This does not establish VoiceOver usability or new macOS 26 visual
acceptance, and does not waive failures found on the physical acceptance Mac.
