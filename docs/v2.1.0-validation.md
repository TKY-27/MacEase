## Final candidate results

Production source is frozen at signed candidate 5. All 50 changed code/build
files match its archived source hashes. Build 10, strict lint 10 and all 1,205
device tests pass. Three fresh GPT-6.1 Sol/high review rounds and targeted
follow-ups found no remaining material source defect. Acceptance remains
incomplete because the measured latency and actual-app memory gates below
are not met; these targets have not been waived.

The final matrix contains 108 full-resolution candidate records and 12 paired
default-policy records, three trials per case. One already-completed default
photo trial is reused only with matching frozen binary and input hashes.
Historical baseline records retain the same fixtures and optimization flags.
The real-photo source is NASA ISS072-E-540816, 8,256 × 5,504 (about 45.4 MP),
center-cropped/resampled for the fixture dimensions. The 48 MP case includes
a slight upscale and is not a native 48 MP camera capture. The other fixtures
cover synthetic texture, actual CoreText English/Japanese text, hard-edged
patterns, transparency and gradients.
All 120 working copies retained their input SHA-256. All 54 final PNG
conversions passed independent full-frame ImageIO pixel equality. Skips and
expected full-resolution target failures are excluded from success timings.

For 24 MP photography, PNG conversion improves from a 12.000-second median
to 1.539 seconds without changing decoded pixels. Some 48 MP conversions
spend 1–3 seconds more because they now perform optional lossless optimization
that the baseline skipped; they are not described as conversion speedups.

The 48 MP default photograph produces 9,709,855 bytes at 5,832 × 4,374. Its
output bytes and independently rendered pixels equal the baseline. The default
gradient produces 9,080,347 bytes at 7,200 × 5,400, with baseline-identical
rendered pixels. The speedups do not come from extra loss of resolution or
palette quality. Full-resolution photographic-fixture SSIM is 0.982930 at 12 MP and
0.978088 at 24 MP, exceeding the existing 0.97 fixture gate. The resized 48 MP
reference scores 0.972627; this measures encoding fidelity at output dimensions,
not preservation of the original resolution. The artificial alpha/gradient
stress fixtures retain visible palette/dither texture; their metrics are not
presented as photographic quality passes. No source-quality metric regression
was detected. The 24 MP alpha palette fix reduces chroma error from 0.023384
to 0.012490 and retains 256 rather than 55 actual palette colors.

The parent visually inspected unresampled 512-pixel crops of the final 24 MP
photo, text, alpha and gradient cases and the 48 MP default photo/gradient
outputs. Cloud detail and text edges remain readable. Alpha tinting is reduced;
palette texture remains visible in stress gradients. The default photo/gradient
crops match their baseline crops. Native-source and output-sized resampled
references are labeled separately in the crop records. These observations
are scoped visual checks, not a claim that lossy output is visually lossless.

Actual signed-app memory remains a failure: the fresh 48 MP photo process
peaks at **1,588,871,168 bytes**, above **1,500,000,000 bytes**, and exits normally
with status zero after successful verification and commit. The serial
alpha/photo process reaches 1,584,463,872 bytes. Standalone benchmark RSS
cannot replace either full-app result. Exact raw records are under
`build/v2.1.0/png-perf/final-summary.json`, `final-default-quality.json` and
`final-quality-summary.json`; host results are in
`build/v2.1.0/host-candidate5-runtime.json`.

## PNG measurements and quality

The first frozen paired matrix contains 216 hashed runs: 18 fixtures across
compression and PNG conversion, with three runs per version. Inputs include
synthetic texture, real photography, rendered Japanese/English text, alpha and
gradients at 12, 24 and 48 MP. All input copies retained their original hashes.
Lossless conversions passed independent full-frame pixel equality. At this
stage, successful lossy outputs also match baseline decoded pixels exactly.

The initial candidate improves 24 MP alpha compression from 31.819 to 8.223
seconds and photographic PNG conversion from 12.000 to 1.609 seconds. It misses
the compression gates for the 24 MP photograph (14.557 seconds), 24 MP gradient
(10.840 seconds) and 48 MP alpha image (61.889 seconds). Unreachable targets
also exposed redundant full search replay for a minimum-size error estimate;
that replay has been removed. These frozen numbers remain diagnostic evidence,
not final-candidate acceptance.

Visual inspection also found strong color posterization in the alpha stress
fixture, present in both the baseline and initial candidate. Baseline equality
alone does not establish acceptable quality. The splitter prematurely stopped all palette generation when one region could
not split. It now continues with other regions. The same independent decoded
alpha-gradient regression fails the old code on both composited color and alpha
error, then passes the fixed code at the unchanged 2% bounds. All 14 focused
Formats and 22 Engine tests pass. Final measurements must retain the source-based quality checks,
including alpha, real photographic detail, text edges and gradients.

After the splitter fix and bounded palette concurrency, single diagnostic
runs complete 24 MP alpha in 8.903 seconds, 24 MP photography in 8.945 seconds,
and 48 MP alpha in 28.463 seconds. The 24 MP alpha output now retains 256 actual
palette entries and fits 9,583,536 bytes. These are single runs, not median
acceptance. The 24 MP gradient still takes 11.233 seconds. A default-policy
48 MP photograph takes 51.193 seconds to reach 9,709,855 bytes at 5,832 × 4,374,
and standalone RSS reaches 1,654,865,920 bytes. Its latency and memory are both
unacceptable. Scoped synchronous autorelease pools around scale generation,
lossless encoding, shared-pixel creation and palette workers reduce a diagnostic
run to 1,098,104,832 bytes with independently identical decoded output. That
prototype was measured while VM work was active and does not establish final
latency or actual-app memory acceptance.

A later uninstrumented standalone run with shared exact pixels and direct
filter-zero rows still reaches 1,769,570,304 bytes in 53.143 seconds, with the
same output bytes and independently identical rendered pixels. This fails the
memory gate. Process mapping summaries show about 500 MiB resident in empty
large-allocation regions. Allocator pressure relief and immediate Core Image
rendering/cache clearing did not resolve it; neither prototype was adopted.
A filter-only mapped workspace prototype lowered RSS to 1,610,137,600 bytes
but still failed. Mapping both filter and compression workspaces lowered a
prototype run to 1,380,696,064 bytes in 49.120 seconds, with the same output
bytes and independent rendered-pixel equality. A self Mach region check
verified release after success, cancellation, partial writes and failure. A
Darwin `mincore` return code alone was an invalid release oracle: it still
returned success/unknown state after a direct successful `munmap`. The proof
was corrected to query readable mappings while retaining byte/cancel checks.
The private mapped workspaces have now been promoted and passed focused
checks and source review. The uninstrumented production harness completes in
49.555 seconds at 1,381,990,400 bytes RSS with byte-identical output. Actual-app
acceptance remains pending.

A bounded follow-up confirmed that the standalone harness already uses full
JobEngine planning, commit and output verification, with the same Release
`-O`/whole-module flags. No missing verification stage or extra UI image decode
was identified. Its instrumented run reached 1,435,041,792 bytes; keep this
separate from uninstrumented timing. App mapping captures were taken after
completion and are recorded as idle evidence, not matching processing phases.
They do not establish a small additional production defect. Source is frozen
for the final three-run matrix, with the actual-app memory failure retained.

The frozen matrix and raw records are in `build/v2.1.0/png-perf/RESULTS.md`,
`frozen-manifest.json` and `paired-events.jsonl`. Standalone benchmark RSS includes
post-engine verification and cannot replace the actual Release-app memory gate.
