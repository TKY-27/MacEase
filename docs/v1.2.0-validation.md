## Audio and video measurements

Independent ffmpeg decoding of actual app outputs measured approximately 440 Hz
and at most 8.34 ms A/V marker error for MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI and FLV at 2x.
FLV's corrected timestamp quantum measured 440.008 Hz and 6.31 ms maximum
marker error. Missing Matroska audio packets retained the intended silence.
These satisfy the fixture's 1% pitch and 20 ms synchronization limits.

A ten-minute video became exactly five minutes for both tracks. Sampled app RSS
rose from 98.5 to 130.1 MiB rather than retaining the 109.9 MiB PCM source.
This is one warm-app observation, not a general memory guarantee.

The Open Speech Repository's OSR_us_000_0010_8k.wav exposed a native AAC bitrate
error. The actual app now creates a six-second H.264/AAC 8 kHz output from its
twelve-second MOV fixture. The owner listened to the original and 2x audio and
reported no unnatural pitch change or dropout. The sample is not bundled.

## Limits and exclusions

- Speed processing accepts mono and stereo audio. Other channel layouts fail
  explicitly rather than being relabeled or silently downmixed.
- This Mac's iCloud folders suppress Finder Sync. The Services fallback is the
  verified route. Initial protected reads could wait despite enabled macOS
  permission switches; explicitly selecting the folder and restarting MacEase
  restored access. No Full Disk Access grant or TCC reset was used. The app
  documents this OS-wait recovery, not a forced interruption of the OS call.
- UNVERIFIED, OWNER-EXCLUDED: actual VoiceOver operation and macOS 26 VM visual
  interaction (ADR 0038). Accessibility labels are not VoiceOver acceptance.
- The release uses the existing self-signed certificate, without Apple
  notarization. No successful GitHub Actions run is claimed.
